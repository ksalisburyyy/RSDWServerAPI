# Verification limits

Prior owner confirmation reported dashboard access, chat/Discord, give, kick/rejoin,
native saves, server controls, backups, shared map/tracking and death notifications;
/help and one command audit on .7, named Owner/Minghoo audits on .8. This source
synchronization does not repeat those live checks or establish universal coverage.

Death records use engine Client_SendDeathEventTelemetry with capture-time identity,
sequence deduplication and freshness. Do not restore the polling-health-edge gate.
Mobs/ranged/environment deaths were reported verified; every boss/cause was not.
Self-instigator is not sufficient to call suicide; falls are Fall damage and
unknown attribution remains unresolved.

Manual save distinguishes request acceptance from observed completion. Availability
depends on active player/game-thread discovery; joining and discovery retry may be
needed after startup. Compatibility & Recovery retries validated discovery and
exports redacted diagnostics; it does not invent offsets or guarantee future patches.

The ledger keeps 1000 events while counters persist. Full historical analytics,
inventory/equipment inspection/editing, scheduled maintenance/backup retention,
offsite backup automation and comprehensive player-to-NPC kill coverage are unfinished.
Filtering weather warnings does not repair the underlying game warning.
